# PS Fuel 3.5.0 fuel-grade matrix

## Petrol / Benzin
Regular 87, Midgrade 89, Premium 91, Premium 93, Premium 98, Benzin 95, Benzin 98 and Race Fuel 100.

## Ethanol and biofuel
E10, E15, E85 Bioethanol and E100 Bioethanol.

## Diesel and renewable diesel
Diesel, Premium Diesel, ULSD, B5, B20, B100, HVO100, Renewable Diesel R99 and Marine Diesel.

## Aviation
Avgas 100LL, Avgas UL94, Jet A, Jet A-1, SAF 50, SAF 100 and JP-8-style turbine fuel.

## Special
Methanol M100, Nitromethane Race Fuel, RP-1-style Rocket Kerosene and gameplay Rocket Propellant.

## Compatibility
The public GetFuel/SetFuel API remains percentage based. Vehicle profiles store a fuel family; individual grades control price, octane/quality and consumption behavior.
